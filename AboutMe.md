## my semantle strat

### I actually spend a lot of my free time playing word games. Including what I like to call, the "-Le" games. Right now, I played game #1587. Here's my approach:
- For a game like semantle, I'm really not opposed to just running wild. Starting on really random words, and their oppposites. For this one, I started with "jubilee", and jumped to "summit". 
- From there I was kind of at a loss, trying a lot of adjectives that weren't getting me anywhere. I ended up asking for a hint. Frankly, offended that my hint was "biscuits", but it eventually led me to the right spot after ~ 60 guesses.  
- My suggestion to anyone playing a word association game like this, is to start basic yet slightly specific, and then think as broad and big-picture as possible. Try to cover your bases as you go through. If you get a hint like "biscuits", think beyond all those things that categorize a biscuit. 
- Following with an educated broad guess rather than a specific follow up to your best answer is not how I like to play. You might get a red herring because you followed one though too closely. But to each their own. 
- Visualizing a large picture is most important to me, I think tunnel vision hinders a lot of progress whether that be in something as small .
